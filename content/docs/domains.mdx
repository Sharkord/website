Claim up to 3 subdomains like `myserver.shrkrd.site` at [domains.sharkord.com](https://domains.sharkord.com) and point them at your server's IP. Let's Encrypt works on them like on any domain, so follow any [HTTPS guide](/docs/https/why).

Rules:

- Only for Sharkord servers. Anything else gets you banned.
- Abuse is removed without notice.
- Subdomains inactive for more than 2 days are reclaimed.
