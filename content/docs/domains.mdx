Sharkord runs a free subdomain service on `shrkrd.site`, so you can host a server with HTTPS without buying a domain. Handy for testing and for small communities.

## Claim One

Go to [domains.sharkord.com](https://domains.sharkord.com), register, and claim your subdomain. Each account gets up to 3, so you can keep separate ones for testing, friends, and a real server.

Your subdomain looks like `yourname.shrkrd.site`. Pick `myserver` and your instance lives at `myserver.shrkrd.site`.

## Point It at Your Server

When you claim a subdomain, set it to your Sharkord server's public IP address. DNS propagation usually takes a few minutes.

Once it resolves, follow one of the [HTTPS guides](/docs/https/why) to get a certificate for it. Let's Encrypt issues certificates for `shrkrd.site` subdomains like any other domain.

## Rules

- Subdomains are for hosting Sharkord servers. Pointing one at another service gets you banned from the service.
- Subdomains used for abuse, spam, or anything against the terms of service are suspended or removed without notice.
- Subdomains inactive for more than 2 days will be reclaimed and made available to someone else.
- We reserve the right to suspend or remove any subdomain at our discretion.
