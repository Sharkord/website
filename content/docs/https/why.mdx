---
title: Why HTTPS
description: What breaks without it, and which guide to follow.
icon: CircleQuestionMark
---

Browsers only allow the microphone, webcam and screen sharing on HTTPS or `http://localhost`. On plain HTTP, chat works but voice and video do not, and passwords travel unencrypted.

Sharkord does not do TLS itself. Put a reverse proxy in front of port `4991`:

- [Docker Compose](/docs/introduction/installation/docker-compose): Sharkord and Caddy in one file. Recommended.
- [Caddy](./caddy): automatic certificates.
- [Nginx](./nginx) or [Apache](./apache): certificates through certbot.

No domain? Get a [free subdomain](/docs/domains).

Media on port `40000` does not go through the proxy. Keep it open on TCP and UDP.
