---
title: Why Do I Need HTTPS?
description: What breaks without HTTPS, and how to fix it.
icon: CircleQuestionMark
---

Browsers only give a page access to the microphone, webcam, and screen in a [secure context](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MediaDevices/getUserMedia): HTTPS, or `http://localhost`. Over plain HTTP on any other address, those requests are blocked before Sharkord ever sees them.

So without HTTPS:

- Text chat, file sharing, and everything else still work.
- Voice, webcam, and screen sharing do not.
- Traffic between your users and the server is unencrypted, including their passwords.

Testing locally on `http://localhost` is fine. Anything else needs a certificate. See [Voice and Devices](/docs/voice) for what those blocked features are.

### How to Set It Up

Sharkord does not terminate TLS itself. Put a reverse proxy in front of it, point your domain at the server, and let the proxy handle certificates:

- **[Caddy](/docs/https/caddy)**: the easiest option. Certificates are automatic.
- **[Nginx](/docs/https/nginx)**: certificates through certbot.
- **[Apache](/docs/https/apache)**: certificates through certbot.
- **[Docker Compose](/docs/introduction/installation/docker-compose)**: Sharkord and Caddy together in one file.

No domain? Claim a [free subdomain](/docs/domains).

<Alert type="info" title="Media does not go through the proxy">
  The reverse proxy handles HTTP and WebSocket traffic on port 4991 only. Voice
  and video go directly to port `40000` over UDP and TCP, so that port has to
  stay open in your firewall.
</Alert>
