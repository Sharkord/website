Sharkord is a self-hosted communication platform that brings core Discord-like features to your own infrastructure: voice channels, text chat, and file sharing, with full control over your data.

<Alert type="warning" title="Alpha software">
  Sharkord is in alpha. Expect bugs, incomplete features, and breaking changes.
</Alert>

## Why Sharkord?

- Self-hosted and privacy first.
- No third-party lock-in.
- Voice, video, screen sharing, text, and file sharing in one place.
- Ships as a single binary for Linux, Windows, and macOS, or as a Docker image.

Try Sharkord at [demo.sharkord.com](https://demo.sharkord.com).
