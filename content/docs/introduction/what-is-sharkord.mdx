Sharkord is a self-hosted communication platform that brings core Discord-like features to your own infrastructure. It shares the philosophy of TeamSpeak, with a modern interface and a focus on being easy to run.

You host the voice channels, the text chat, and the files. Nothing leaves your machine unless you make it.

### Core Principles

- Self-hosted
- Lightweight
- Clean interface
- No tracking or telemetry
- Open source
- No paywalls or premium tiers
- Easy to deploy

### What You Get

- Voice channels with screen sharing and webcam
- Text channels, threads, replies, reactions, and direct messages
- File and image sharing with per-role storage limits
- Roles, per-channel permissions, and invites
- A [plugin system](/docs/plugins/overview) for anything else

### What It Is Not

Sharkord targets small and medium private communities. It is not trying to replicate every Discord feature, and it is not built to host large public servers.
